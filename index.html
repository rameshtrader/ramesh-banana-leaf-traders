About us

Fresh from the grower, straight to your door

Ramesh Banana Leaf Traders supplies fresh, high-quality banana leaves to homes, temples, event halls and restaurants across Kumbakonam. Every leaf is cut and delivered fresh daily, ready for weddings, poojas, community functions and everyday meals.

We work closely with local growers, so what reaches you is always fresh, clean and traditional — whether you need a few leaves for the kitchen or a bulk order for a large wedding.
